DEFENSE SLIDE IMAGES
Cropped reference figures, ready to drop into the Google Slides deck.
They live in the 'paper figures' subfolder.

Each file is named <slide>-<node>_<author>_<year>_<what>. Put the matching
credit line underneath it on the slide. Under the German right of quotation
(section 51 UrhG) the attribution has to be visible on screen.
