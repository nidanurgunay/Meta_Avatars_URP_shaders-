DEFENSE SLIDE IMAGES
====================

Two folders.

  paper figures/      Figures reproduced from the cited papers.
                      Each needs a credit line on the slide. The reference
                      number on the panel's title line covers it.
                      Named <slide>-<node>_<author>_<year>_<what>.

  technique figures/  Renders from the Unity project. Own work, no credit needed.
                      Named platform_<n>_<name> or
                      technique_<version>_<name>_<platform>.
                      The platform suffix is mixamo, meta_sdk, avaturn or jody.

SOURCES.txt  where every paper figure was cropped from, with page and crop box.
CREDITS.txt  the credit line to put under each paper figure.

Showing one technique across several platforms is the RQ1 evidence: the same
operator, a different pipeline, visibly different output. V5 is the clearest
case, since the Meta SDK version reads a camera-distance derivative instead of
a real depth buffer.

Note: the numeric prefixes on the paper figures follow the slide order at the
time they were cut. Slides have moved since, so trust the author name in the
filename rather than the number.

  study figures/      Plots from this study: the prestudy Borda chart, all
                      rendered prestudy variants, and the six user-study
                      result figures. Own work, no credit needed.
