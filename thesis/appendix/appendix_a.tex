%% Appendix A
\chapter{Pre-Study Calibration Results}
\label{app:prestudy_results}

This appendix provides the full results from the pre-study parameter calibration described in \Cref{sec:prestudy_calibration}. The exercise consisted of five sequential ranking rounds followed by a single final preference question. Figure~\ref{fig:prestudy_rounds} shows the per-round first-preference distributions, and Figure~\ref{fig:prestudy_overall} shows the overall visual appeal votes from the final question. All rendered variants shown to participants appear in Figure~\ref{fig:prestudy_all} in the main text.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.90\textwidth]{prestudy_round_results}
  \caption{Per-round first-preference distributions from the pre-study ranking exercise. Each bar shows the percentage of valid participants who ranked that configuration first (most trustworthy) within its round. Options are sorted by this percentage, and all panels share the same 0--100\,\% scale to allow direct visual comparison. The gold bar marks the top-ranked configuration in each round. Options labelled with an asterisk in Rounds~4 and~5 correspond to the carry-forward baseline from Round~1 (R1E, inner line threshold 0.05, no additional post-processing). In both rounds the baseline was preferred by a large majority, and those effects were consequently excluded from the final composite.}
  \label{fig:prestudy_rounds}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.80\textwidth]{prestudy_overall_appeal}
  \caption{Overall visual appeal votes from the final question of the pre-study. After completing all five rounds, participants were asked to name the single avatar they found most visually appealing across the entire study. Bars show raw vote counts out of 36 valid responses (9 participants left this question blank). One participant selected R4~A, which is identical to R1~E and is merged into that count. R1~E received the most votes overall, confirming the selection made on the basis of the per-round trustworthiness rankings.}
  \label{fig:prestudy_overall}
\end{figure}
