\section*{Parameter Adjustment Study Results}
\label{app:prestudy_results}

The figures below present the full ranking data from the five-round parameter
adjustment study described in \Cref{sec:prestudy_calibration}. Each bar shows the
percentage of participants who assigned a given option to that rank position and
options within each round are ordered by Borda score, highest first. The Borda
score for each option is the sum of $(N - r + 1)$ across all valid responses,
where $N$ is the number of options in that round and $r$ the rank position
assigned by a participant. An option placed first by every participant therefore
scores $N$ times the number of valid responses. A higher Borda score indicates a
more consistently preferred option across the full distribution. The mean rank
(MR) is reported alongside each bar, where a lower value indicates higher
preference.
